Recognizing and Responding to Shock

Early warning signs of shock and immediate stabilizing actions.

Early signs

Pale or clammy skin, rapid breathing, confusion, and a weak or fast pulse can all point to shock — often before it's obvious the person is seriously hurt.

Immediate steps

Keep the person lying down, treat any visible bleeding, keep them warm, and avoid giving food or water in case they need surgery. Get them to medical care as fast as the situation allows.
