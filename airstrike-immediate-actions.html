Airstrike Safety

Immediate Actions During and After an Airstrike

What to do in the moment, and the safety checks to run once it's over.

During

Get low and take cover away from windows and unreinforced structures. Stay down until there's been a clear pause, not just a lull.

After

Check yourself and others for injuries before moving. Watch for unexploded ordnance in the strike area — treat it the same as suspected mined ground and do not approach debris.

Immediate sequence — general practice, adapt to your site security plan
StepAction
1Get low, move away from windows/glass
2Move to reinforced shelter if reachable in seconds, otherwise stay low where you are
3Wait for a clear, sustained pause before moving
4Check self and others for injury; apply first aid as needed
5Do not approach debris or unfamiliar objects — treat as possible UXO
