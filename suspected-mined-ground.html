What to Do If You Suspect Mined Ground

Immediate actions to take, and who to notify, if you believe you are near a hazard.

Immediate steps

Stop moving. Do not turn around and retrace your steps unless you are certain of the exact path you walked in on — step exactly where you have already stepped. Alert others nearby without causing them to move suddenly.

After you're clear

Report the location as precisely as possible to your security focal point so the area can be marked and avoided by others.
