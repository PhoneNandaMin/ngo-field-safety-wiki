Recognizing Landmine and UXO Indicators

Visual and environmental clues that an area may be mined or contaminated, drawing on mine-risk education practice from organizations like the HALO Trust and ICRC.

What to look for

Common indicators — not exhaustive; local community knowledge always takes priority
IndicatorWhat it may mean
Painted rocks, crossed sticks, tied clothInformal community-placed warning markers
Damaged vehicles or animal carcasses off-pathPossible prior detonation in the area
Areas visibly avoided by local residentsLocal knowledge of contamination, even if unmarked
Overgrown or untouched fields near active onesLand abandoned due to known or suspected hazard

General practice

Stick to roads and paths that are in regular, visible use, and treat any area described by locals as unsafe as unsafe — local knowledge is usually more current than any map.
