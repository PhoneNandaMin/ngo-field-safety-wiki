Two-Factor Authentication

Why a second login step matters, and how to set it up on the accounts that matter most.

Why it matters

A password alone can be guessed, reused, or stolen. Two-factor authentication (2FA) requires a second proof of identity — usually a code from an app — so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get into an account.

Where to start

Prioritize email first, since it's usually the recovery path into everything else, then messaging apps and any account tied to donor or beneficiary data.
