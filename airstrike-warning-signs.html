Recognizing Airstrike Warning Signs

Early indicators that may precede an airstrike, and how to interpret them.

What to watch for

Aircraft circling or flying low and repeatedly over the same area, sudden movement of local residents toward shelter, and reports from community networks are all signs worth acting on immediately rather than waiting to confirm.

General practice

Know your nearest shelter option before you need it, and keep a way to receive real-time local alerts (community radio, messaging groups) rather than relying on official channels alone.
